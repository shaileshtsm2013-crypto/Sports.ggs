# Official IKF / Pro Kabaddi Rules Summary

## Court Dimensions
- **Men**: 13m x 10m divided into two 6.5m x 10m halves by the Midline.
- **Women / Juniors**: 12m x 8m.
- **Baulk Line**: 3.75m from the Midline.
- **Bonus Line**: 4.75m from the Midline (1m past the Baulk Line).
- **Lobby**: 1m yellow boundary strip on both sides activated only after a touch.

## Match Duration
- Two halves of 20 minutes each with a 5-minute interval.
- 7 active players on court per team (up to 5 substitutes).

## Raid Mechanics
- A raid lasts a maximum of 30 seconds.
- The raider must chant the continuous audible cant ("Kabaddi, Kabaddi") in a single breath without breaks.
- A raid is valid only if the raider crosses the Baulk line with at least one foot in the air while the other is across, or makes a touch.
- **Bonus Point**: Awarded if the raider crosses the bonus line with one foot trailing in the air while 6 or more defenders are on court.
- **Super Raid**: A raid where the raider scores 3 or more points in a single raid.
- **Super Tackle**: When 3 or fewer defenders successfully tackle the raider, earning 2 points instead of 1.
- **All Out (Lona)**: When all 7 players of a team are dismissed, the opposing team is awarded 2 bonus points and all players revive.
