# Official FIVB Volleyball Rules Summary

## Court & Equipment
- **Dimensions**: 18m x 9m court divided into two 9m x 9m halves by a center net.
- **Attack Line**: Marked 3m parallel to the centerline on both sides.
- **Net Height**: 2.43m for Men, 2.24m for Women.

## Playing Format & Rotation
- 6 players on the court per team at all times.
- Players rotate clockwise whenever they win a rally on the opponent's serve.
- Positions: 1 (Back-Right / Server), 2 (Front-Right), 3 (Front-Center), 4 (Front-Left), 5 (Back-Left), 6 (Back-Center).

## Ball Handling Rules
- Maximum 3 contacts per side (excluding a block touch).
- A single player cannot hit the ball twice consecutively (except immediately following a block).
- The ball must be hit cleanly—no catching, lifting, or throwing (lift/carry fault).

## Scoring
- Rally Point System: A point is scored on every rally regardless of who served.
- Sets 1–4 are played to 25 points (must win by 2).
- Set 5 (tiebreak) is played to 15 points (must win by 2).
- Match is best of 5 sets.
