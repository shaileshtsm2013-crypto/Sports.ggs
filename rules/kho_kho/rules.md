# Official Kho Kho Federation of India (KKFI) Rules Summary

## Pitch & Court Dimensions
- **Court**: 27m long x 16m wide rectangular area.
- **Central Lane**: 24m long x 30cm wide running down the center between two wooden posts.
- **Cross Lanes**: 8 cross lanes (each 16m long x 30cm wide) intersecting the central lane perpendicularly.
- **Squares**: 8 squares (30cm x 30cm) formed at intersections where 8 chasers sit in alternating facing directions.
- **Posts**: Two cylindrical wooden posts (1.20m–1.25m height) fixed at either end of the central lane.
- **Free Zone**: D-shaped / rectangular area behind each post (1.5m) where chasers can change direction freely.

## Team Composition & Match Format
- 12 players per team (9 active players on court: 8 sitting chasers + 1 active chaser; 3 defending runners enter in batches).
- A match consists of 2 innings. Each inning consists of a Chase Turn and a Running Turn (9 minutes each for seniors).

## Core Rules & Direction Changing
- **Early Direction**: Once an active chaser takes a direction (towards either post), they cannot turn back or cross the central lane unless they enter the Free Zone behind the post.
- **Giving Kho**: An active chaser touches a sitting teammate on the back while loudly pronouncing "Kho". The sitting teammate then becomes the active chaser.
- **Cross Lane Touch**: Chasers cannot cross the central lane directly; they must run around the post to switch sides or give 'Kho'.
- **Runners (Defenders)**: 3 runners enter the field per batch and attempt to avoid being tagged by using agility and tactical dodging.
